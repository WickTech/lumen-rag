# Austin office
The Austin office hosts sales, customer success and the American support team, with 90 desks and 6 meeting rooms. It is open from 07:30 to 19:00 on weekdays. A demo area with an H200 robot cell is available to customers by appointment, booked at least 2 working days ahead. Parking is provided in the underground garage and there is no prototyping lab at this site.
