# On-call stipend
Engineers on call receive a flat weekly stipend of 300 euros. In addition, each page that is classified as Sev1 pays a bonus of 120 euros, and each Sev2 page pays 40 euros, provided the engineer joins the incident call within the response time. Sev3 and Sev4 pages carry no bonus. The stipend and bonuses are paid with the next payroll and are funded from the owning team's budget.
