# H400 heavy-duty robot specifications
The H400 is the heavy-duty model for pallet handling and requires aisles of at least 2.0 metres. It carries up to 120 kilograms, travels at a maximum of 1.4 metres per second and lifts to 4.5 metres. The battery capacity is 3.2 kilowatt-hours, giving about 10 hours of operation. The H400 adds a 3D lidar and a rear safety scanner that the H200 does not have.
