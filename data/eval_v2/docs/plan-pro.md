# Pro plan
The Pro plan costs 7,900 euros per month and supports up to 150 robots across at most five sites. It adds webhooks, single sign-on through SAML, custom reports and an API limit of 600 requests per minute. Support is available by email and chat. Pro customers can buy extra robot capacity in blocks of 25 robots at 1,100 euros per month per block. Annual billing gives two months free.
